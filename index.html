Research

Value-Oriented Thematic Learning Design
Master's Thesis · 2024–2027

Investigating how value-oriented themes can be systematically designed to support value education, with particular attention to moral dilemma scenarios, value conflicts, and learners' value-based decision making. The project develops and iteratively refines a reusable methodology for value-oriented thematic learning design.

The Limitations of Generative Artificial Intelligence in Instructional Design
Research Project · 2025–2026

Investigating the pedagogical quality and practical usability of generative AI in instructional design through a qualitative comparison between AI-generated and human-designed lesson plans. The project examines learning objectives, goal–method alignment, temporal structure, and teacher–student interaction.

Professional Identity and Reconstruction Among Researchers in the AI Era
Course Research Project · 2024–2025

Examining how the development of AI shapes professional identity among researchers in Chinese higher education through qualitative interviews and thematic analysis.

Educational Big Data and Learning Analytics for Personalized Learning
Undergraduate Thesis · 2023–2024

Designed and implemented an educational data analytics system for analyzing students' learning and behavioral data to support personalized learning and data-informed instructional decision making.
