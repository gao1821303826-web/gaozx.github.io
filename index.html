About

I am a master's student in Educational Technology at Beijing Normal University, with a bachelor's background in Software Engineering. My research interests lie at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED), instructional design, and human-centered technology. I approach educational research from a design perspective, viewing educational systems as complex systems that can be systematically constructed through design technologies, rather than relying solely on intuition or experience. My work focuses on developing operational and verifiable approaches to the design of learning environments, instructional processes, and educational technologies, with particular interests in AI-supported teaching and learning, learning design, curriculum development, teacher professional development, and AI literacy. I am also interested in the relationship among AI, humans, and values, particularly how ethical, moral, and other value considerations can be incorporated into the design of AI-mediated educational systems.
