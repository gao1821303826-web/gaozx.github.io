About

I am a master's student in Educational Technology at Beijing Normal University, with a bachelor's background in Software Engineering. My research interests lie at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED), instructional design, and human-centered technology. I approach educational research from a design perspective, viewing educational systems as complex systems that can be systematically constructed through design technologies, rather than relying solely on intuition or experience. My work focuses on developing operational and verifiable approaches to the design of learning environments, instructional processes, and educational technologies, with particular interests in AI-supported teaching and learning, learning design, curriculum development, teacher professional development, and AI literacy. I am also interested in the relationship among AI, humans, and values, particularly how ethical, moral, and other value considerations can be incorporated into the design of AI-mediated educational systems.

02

Educational Big Data–Based Student Personalized Information Collection System

Educational Big Data · Personalized Learning · Data Visualization
Project image / video

An educational data system designed to collect, process, and visualize large-scale student behavioral data to support personalized learning and data-informed educational decision-making.

Key Features

  • Collection and integration of student learning and behavioral data from multiple sources.
  • Large-scale data cleaning, transformation, aggregation, and analysis.
  • Interactive dashboards and reports for exploring student characteristics and learning-related trends.
  • Data analysis supporting personalized learning and educational decision-making.
Technology
Hadoop Spark Flume Kafka SQL Apache Superset Linux VMware

The system was deployed in a distributed Linux environment using three Ubuntu virtual machines. A Flume–Kafka–Flume data pipeline was implemented for data transmission and processing, with SQL-based data cleaning, transformation, and aggregation applied to more than one million student behavioral records.

03

Earth Revolution and Rotation VR Learning Environment

Unity · Virtual Reality · 3D Visualization · Interactive Learning
Project video / screenshots

An interactive VR learning environment developed with Unity for visualizing Earth's rotation, revolution, axial tilt, seasons, time zones, and climate zones.

Key Features

  • Interactive visualization of Earth's orbital motion and its relationship with seasonal changes.
  • Visualization of spring, summer, autumn, and winter, together with perihelion and aphelion.
  • Interactive control of Earth's rotation speed to explore the relationship between rotation and the passage of time.
  • Visualization of Earth's rotational axis, North and South Poles, and axial tilt.
  • Time-zone boundaries and major thermal zones, including tropical, temperate, and polar zones.
  • Adjustable viewpoints, including elevated and top-down perspectives, for observing Earth's motion from different angles.
Technology
Unity C# 3D Visualization Virtual Reality
04

Educational Knowledge Graph & GraphRAG

Knowledge Graph · GraphRAG · Neo4j · LLM
Project image / architecture diagram

A knowledge-based educational AI system combining an educational knowledge graph with GraphRAG to support structured knowledge retrieval and context-aware question answering.

Key Features

  • Construction of an educational knowledge graph representing relationships among concepts and learning-related knowledge.
  • Deployment and management of a Neo4j graph database in a Docker-based environment.
  • Graph-based retrieval for structured and semantically connected educational knowledge.
  • Integration of graph-based retrieval with large language models for context-aware responses.

GraphRAG Knowledge Graph Neo4j Docker LLM Graph Database
