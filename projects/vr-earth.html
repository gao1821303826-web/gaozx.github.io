Earth Revolution and Rotation VR

Unity · Virtual Reality · 3D Visualization · Learning Environment

Earth Revolution and Rotation VR is an interactive learning environment developed with Unity to visualize geographical and astronomical concepts that are difficult to understand through static diagrams alone.

The environment allows learners to explore the Earth's movement through interactive 3D visualization and user-controlled perspectives. The project combines virtual reality, spatial visualization, and interactive controls to support conceptual understanding of Earth's motion and related geographical phenomena.

Learning Features

  • Visualization of Earth's revolution and rotation.
  • Exploration of the relationship between Earth's motion and the four seasons.
  • Visualization of perihelion and aphelion.
  • Interactive representation of Earth's axis, North and South Poles, and axial tilt.
  • Visualization of the angle between the equatorial plane and the ecliptic plane.
  • Interactive time-zone lines and thermal zones, including tropical, temperate, and polar regions.
  • User-controlled viewing perspectives during revolution, including elevated and top-down perspectives.
  • Interactive control of Earth's rotation speed during the rotation mode.

Technologies
